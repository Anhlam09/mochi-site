What Mochi does

  • ⏰ Break reminders

    Gentle 20-20-20 eye breaks, Pomodoro, or your own timer. Mochi yawns when a break is near and bounces when it's time.

  • 💬 Talks to you

    Good morning hellos, check-ins, kind words and cute fun facts. Tap Mochi and it answers back.

  • 🎨 Six flavors

    Every couple of hours Mochi does a little spin and changes color: strawberry, matcha, taro, ramune, peach or banana.

  • 🔋 Easy on battery

    Calm animation uses about 1% CPU. Mochi stays quiet while you're away.

  • 🔒 Private

    No internet, no accounts, no tracking. Everything stays on your Mac.

  • 🌙 Fits in

    Light and dark mode, four sizes, Reduce Motion, and it never steals focus.

How to install

  1. Download the zip and double-click it to unzip.
  2. Drag Mochi into your Applications folder.
  3. Open Mochi. Your Mac will say it can't verify the developer. That's expected: Mochi is a free hobby app that isn't registered with Apple, which costs $99 a year. Click Done.
  4. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to Mochi. Confirm with your password.
  5. That's it! Look for 🌱 in your menu bar. You only need to do this once.

On macOS 14 Sonoma you can instead right-click Mochi, choose Open, then click Open again.

Check your download (optional)

Only download Mochi from this page. To make sure your file is the real one, open Terminal and run:

shasum -a 256 ~/Downloads/Mochi-0.1.0.zip

It should print exactly this fingerprint:

32c5275ad6855f53ddcb9aefbeb1c5c48a65ec7f5b483956e6fbe3666c3cf955

If it's different, don't open the file.
